### **⚡ Final Step: Update Vercel Environment**

**To complete the deployment:**

2. **Select your project**: `smartchainhubfrontend`

4. **Update**: `NEXT_PUBLIC_AI_AGENT_URL` = `https://smartchain-hub.onrender.com`
5. **Redeploy** → Trigger new deployment

