## Overview

The $SCH token is the native utility and governance token of the SmartChain Hub protocol. It aligns incentives between four groups: **users** (who generate transaction data), **stakers** (who provide capital and earn yield), **agent owners** (who list trained agents on the hire-market), and **protocol contributors** (who build the ecosystem).

$SCH is not a speculative asset — it is a claim on real protocol revenue generated by AI optimization fees, agent hire commissions, and escrow transaction fees.

## Token Utility

### 1. Protocol Fee Discounts
Users who hold or stake $SCH receive discounts on the 0.5% optimization fee:

| $SCH Staked | Discount | Effective Fee |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 999 | 0% | 0.50% |
| 1,000 – 9,999 | 20% | 0.40% |
| 10,000 – 99,999 | 40% | 0.30% |
| 100,000+ | 60% | 0.20% |

### 2. Staking Yield
$SCH holders can stake tokens in `SmartChainPayments.sol` to earn:
- Base APY: **5%** in $SCH
- Boosted APY: up to **12%** when protocol revenue exceeds $100K/month
- Revenue share: proportional claim on 0.5% optimization fees and 10% AgentMarket commissions

### 3. Agent Market Governance
$SCH stakers vote on:
- AgentMarket protocol fee (currently 10%) — adjustable 5–20%
- Minimum agent listing price
- Whitelist/blacklist of agent model hashes
- Protocol treasury fund allocations

### 4. Agent Market Priority
Agent listings backed by $SCH stake receive:
- Priority placement in `getActiveListings()` sort order
- "Verified Agent" badge requiring 10,000 $SCH staked
- Enhanced reputation score weighting in the marketplace

### 5. Fine-Tune Credits
Each $SCH grants 1 fine-tune credit per epoch (30 days). Credits are burned on use. Users with no $SCH can purchase credits at 0.01 A0GI per credit.

## Protocol Revenue Model

SmartChain Hub generates revenue from three independent streams:

```
Stream 1 — AI Optimization Fee
  0.5% of every optimized transaction amount
  → 90% to stakers (SmartChainRevenue)
  → 10% to Protocol Treasury

Stream 2 — Agent Hire-Market Commission
  10% of every agent hire payment
  → 70% to Protocol Treasury
  → 30% to $SCH stakers (additional yield boost)

Stream 3 — Agent Escrow Fee
  1% of every agent-to-agent escrow withdrawal
  → 100% to Protocol Treasury
```

## Governance

$SCH uses a simple, gas-efficient governance model:

**Quorum:** 4% of circulating supply must vote for a proposal to pass.
**Majority:** Simple majority (>50%) for parameter changes; supermajority (67%) for treasury releases.
**Timelock:** All passed proposals execute after a 48-hour timelock (security buffer).
**Delegation:** Token holders can delegate votes without transferring tokens.

**Governable parameters:**
- Protocol fee rates (all three streams)
- Staking APY parameters
- Agent verification requirements
- Grant allocations from Ecosystem Fund
- Addition of new protocol modules

---

## Anti-Dilution Protections

1. **Fixed supply**: No minting after TGE. Inflation rate = 0%.
2. **Buy-and-burn**: 20% of Protocol Treasury revenue used to buy $SCH on open market and burn.
3. **Revenue-backed floor**: Protocol commits to maintaining a treasury reserve ≥ 6 months of operating costs.
4. **Investor lock-up**: All seed investors subject to 6-month cliff — no immediate dump at TGE.
