## Delivery Notes

```
PACE
  Speak at 130–140 words per minute — deliberate, not rushed.
  Pause 1 full second after every technical term (TEE, Merkle root, soulbound).
  The silence after the closing line is intentional — do not fill it.

TONE
  Confident. Not excited. You built something real — you know it works.
  Do not say "basically", "kind of", "sort of", or "you can see here".
  Every sentence should state a fact, not explain a concept.

SCREEN
  Always have the URL bar visible — judges will pause the video to check.
  Do real actions — do not skip any click or spinner.
  Show ChainScan after every on-chain action — it is your strongest proof.

AUDIO
  No background music — judges need to hear every word clearly.
  Record in a quiet room. One take is ideal. Two maximum.

UPLOAD
  YouTube: Set to Unlisted (not Private — judges need the link to work).
  Loom: Copy the share link, not the edit link.
  Paste the link into HackQuest submission immediately after upload.
```
