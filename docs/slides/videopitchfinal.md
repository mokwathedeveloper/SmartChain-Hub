f# SmartChain Hub — 3-Minute Video Pitch Script
### 0G APAC Hackathon 2026 · Track 2: Agentic Trading Arena

---

> **Before you record:**
> - Record in one take if possible. Authentic beats polished.
> - Sit or stand — whichever feels natural. No pacing.
> - Light your face. Dark background preferred. Laptop glow works.
> - Speak slower than you think you need to. Video always feels faster.
> - Eye contact = look directly into the camera lens, not the screen.
> - Target pace: 130 words per minute. Total words: ~390.

## Recording Checklist

- [ ] Camera at eye level — not looking up or down
- [ ] Face well-lit — no harsh shadows
- [ ] Background dark and clean — or blurred
- [ ] Audio — use earbuds as a mic if no external mic available
- [ ] No background noise — record in a quiet room
- [ ] Do a 30-second test recording before the full take
- [ ] Record 2–3 takes — use the one where you feel most natural
